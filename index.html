About

A stylist who explains every cut.

Bilal Ali is a men's hairstylist from Lebanon. Online, he is known for short, practical videos that answer the questions men actually ask about their hair: whether caps cause hair loss, if serum is worth it, which cut suits your face.

In the chair he works the same way. He looks closely, tells you what will work and what won't, and cuts with precision. You leave with a cut that suits you and a clear idea of how to keep it looking right.

Services

Cuts and care, tailored to you.

Every service starts with a short consultation. Message for prices and availability.

  • Signature cut

    A tailored cut planned around your face shape, hair type and daily routine.

  • Long hair & layers

    Shape, movement and weight removal for men growing their hair, without losing the length.

  • Fades & tapers

    Clean, blended fades, from a soft taper to a sharp skin fade.

  • Beard shaping

    Line-up, trim and shape that frames your jaw and matches your cut.

  • Hair & scalp care

    Advice and treatments for thinning, dryness and damage, with products that actually help.

  • Styling lesson

    Learn to style your cut at home: the right product, the right amount, in under five minutes.

Not sure what you need? Book a consultation and decide together in the chair.
